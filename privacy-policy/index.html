Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Balaji AI Lab (an extension of Balaji Cyber World, located in Bodarwar, Kaptanganj, Kushinagar, Uttar Pradesh 274149, India) collects, stores, uses, and safeguards information when you visit our website at balajiailab.com, submit a demo request, or deploy our software tools.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to fulfill our technical and business services:

  • Direct Inquiry Data: When you submit our on-site demo booking form or message us via WhatsApp or phone, we collect your name, mobile phone number, business type, and specified city/town.
  • Software Configuration Data: For clients deploying our billing software or MealQR, we store store configuration details such as menu item names, HSN codes, and pricing purely to facilitate your system setup.
  • Technical Web Logs: Our web server logs standard connection parameters (IP addresses, browser types, requesting URLs) solely for performance diagnostics and security protection.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for:

  • Contacting you to schedule software demonstrations or deliver requested project quotes.
  • Providing on-site technical support and training at your business premises.
  • Sending critical software patch updates and operational notices.

We never sell, rent, or trade your contact details or business records to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. Data Ownership & Local Sovereignty

We operate with an offline-first philosophy. Your store customer lists, daily sales invoices, and financial records reside directly on your local hardware terminal or dedicated VPS instance. You maintain 100% legal ownership of your business data at all times.

4. Third-Party Integrations

When you request integrations with third-party providers (such as Meta's WhatsApp Business Cloud API, payment gateways, or Google Maps), your data is handled in accordance with their respective compliance policies.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) across our web endpoints, maintain firewall protections on our VPS nodes, and restrict administrative database access to verified technical personnel.
