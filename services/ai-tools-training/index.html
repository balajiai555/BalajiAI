Why Do Theoretical AI Courses Fail Small Business Owners?

Most online artificial intelligence courses focus on complex Python math, machine learning algorithms, or theoretical data models. A retail merchant, sweet shop owner, school principal, or local accountant in eastern UP does not need to learn tensor math—they need to know:

  • How to draft professional vendor emails and quotation letters in 30 seconds.
  • How to generate 20 festive offer banners and WhatsApp promotional captions in Hindi and English.
  • How to summarize long PDF agreements or government tax circulars into simple action points.
  • How to avoid AI hallucinations and verify accuracy before publishing or sending.

Our training curriculum is entirely hands-on. Participants bring their own laptops or smartphones and work on real business tasks during the session.

What Modules Are Covered in the Workshop?

We offer structured, beginner-friendly modules tailored to commercial use cases:

Module 1: Practical Prompt Engineering for Small Business

Moving beyond single-line questions. We teach the Role-Context-Task-Constraint framework, enabling attendees to instruct AI models to adopt the voice of a professional local merchant and output formatted tables or numbered checklists.

Module 2: Customer Support & WhatsApp Communication

How to build reusable prompt templates to answer customer price objections, draft polite payment reminder messages, and write personalized review responses for Google Maps.

Module 3: Document Extraction & Office Productivity

Using AI models to extract tables from messy invoices, format customer addresses for printing, draft staff warning notices or appointment letters, and clean up spreadsheet data.

Module 4: Fact-Checking & Safety Guardrails

Understanding when AI tools make up facts, how to cross-reference data, how to safeguard customer phone numbers and privacy, and how to stay fully compliant with commercial norms.
