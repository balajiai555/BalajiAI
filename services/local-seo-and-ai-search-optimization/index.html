What Is AI Search Optimization (GEO/AEO)?

Search behavior is evolving rapidly. While traditional searchers click on blue links on Google, millions of customers now use AI answer engines like ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews to ask complex questions such as:

"What is a reliable restaurant billing software provider in Kushinagar that offers in-person installation and offline support?"

AI engines do not rank websites based on keyword stuffing. Instead, they parse structured facts, verifiable citations, clear schema data, and factual consistency across the web. If your business information is missing or contradictory across directories, AI tools simply will not cite you.

What Steps Do We Take to Optimize Your Business?

Our technical search program combines local Google Maps optimization with AI-search visibility:

1. Complete Google Business Profile (GBP) Overhaul

We claim, verify, and systematically configure your Google Business Profile: selecting primary and secondary categories, uploading geotagged storefront photos, configuring service areas (Kushinagar, Gorakhpur, Deoria), and establishing a direct review collection link.

2. NAP Consistency Across Local Citations

Search engines require strict consistency for Name, Address, and Phone number (NAP). We audit and align your listing across Justdial, IndiaMART, TradeIndia, and regional commercial registries so search bots recognize your physical existence.

3. Structured Data (JSON-LD) Implementation

We add comprehensive JSON-LD schemas to your website (such as LocalBusiness, PostalAddress, GeoCoordinates, and FAQPage). This tells search crawlers exactly what you offer, where you are located, and how to verify your identity.

4. Entity Optimization for AI Engines

We format your website copy to provide direct, 40-to-60-word answers to typical customer questions, complete with neutral comparison tables, pricing disclosures, and author bios that AI models can extract cleanly.
