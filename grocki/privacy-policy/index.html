1. Introduction

Grocki ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, disclosed, and protected when you use our mobile application (Grocki - Fast & Fresh Grocery Delivery), website (https://balajiailab.com), or related fulfillment services.

By accessing, downloading, or using Grocki, you agree to the collection and use of your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not use our application or services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information strictly necessary to fulfill your grocery delivery orders, authenticate sessions, and maintain your account:

  • Account Information: Your name and email address collected during registration or when using Google Sign-In authentication.
  • Contact Information: Your mobile phone number, used exclusively for delivery driver coordination, order status updates, and SMS/WhatsApp delivery alerts.
  • Delivery Address & Location: Street address, landmarks, and GPS coordinates used solely to deliver your orders to your doorstep and verify serviceability in your neighborhood.
  • Order & Purchase History: Products ordered, order totals, timestamp, and selected payment method (UPI or Cash on Delivery). We do not store bank passwords, UPI PINs, or credit/debit card numbers.
  • Photos & Media (Admins Only): Device image gallery access is used strictly by store administrators to crop, compress, and upload product catalog images. We do not access customer personal photos or camera rolls.
  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, application build number, and network connectivity used solely for app stability, performance monitoring, and bug troubleshooting.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for legitimate business, fulfillment, and operational purposes:

  • Processing, packing, and doorstep delivery of your grocery orders in a timely manner.
  • Sending real-time order status updates, delivery coordination, and driver arrival notifications.
  • Authenticating user sessions and safeguarding your account against unauthorized or fraudulent access.
  • Improving catalog navigation, category search experience, and app loading speeds.
  • Preventing fraudulent orders, spam abuse, and maintaining platform security and integrity.

4. Location Data Policy

Grocki accesses device location (approximate or precise GPS coordinates) only with your explicit in-app permission to detect your delivery address, calculate estimated delivery times, and confirm store serviceability in your area.

Important Zero-Background Tracking Guarantee: Grocki never tracks your location in the background when the app is closed or minimized. Location data is only fetched while you are actively selecting or confirming a delivery address on the checkout or address screen.

You can revoke location permissions at any time through your Android device settings (Settings > Apps > Grocki > Permissions > Location > Don't allow). If disabled, you can still place orders by entering your complete address manually.

5. Data Storage & Service Providers

Zero Sale of Data: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data or phone number to any third parties, data brokers, or advertisers under any circumstances.

We use secure, enterprise-grade cloud hosting and infrastructure providers to store customer and order information. All data stored with our service providers is encrypted at rest and in transit, and processed strictly on our behalf under confidential data protection standards to operate the service.

Financial Security: Payments made via UPI are processed directly through your installed UPI applications (e.g., Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) using secure UPI Intent protocols. We do not intercept, view, or store your UPI PIN, banking credentials, or card details.

6. Data Security Safeguards

We implement multi-layered industry-standard technical and operational safeguards to protect your personal information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the Grocki mobile app and backend servers are strictly encrypted using HTTPS / TLS (Transport Layer Security). Cleartext HTTP traffic is completely blocked.
  • Password Hashing: User account passwords are encrypted using salted cryptographic hashing algorithms and are never stored in plain text.
  • Local Backup Protection: Android application backups (android:allowBackup="false") are disabled to prevent unauthorized local extraction of authentication tokens or user cache.
  • Restricted Access: Only authorized operational staff who require your delivery details to complete an active order have access to customer shipping information.
